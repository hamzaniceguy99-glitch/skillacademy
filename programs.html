The programs, in detail

Four programs of 8 to 16 weeks, each starting from a diagnostic rather than a syllabus.

Program 01 · Fractions, decimals, percentages

Rebuilding the basics

Nearly every adult who struggles with math has a gap around fractions and proportional reasoning. We go back there, rebuild it with methods that make sense rather than tricks to memorize, and practice ten minutes a day until it's automatic.

  • 16 weeks
  • Any starting point
  • 10 min a day
  • Paper and a pen

Program 02 · Letters are just numbers

Algebra without fear

Algebra is where most adults gave up, usually because it arrived as rules with no meaning. We build it from balance and patterns, work on translating words into equations — the part that's genuinely hard — and practice until the fear fades.

  • 12 weeks
  • Basics in place
  • 20 min a day
  • Paper and a pen

Start this program

Program 03 · Confidence in meetings

Numbers at work

For people whose jobs involve numbers they were never taught to handle. We work with your real figures — invoices, margins, dosage conversions, measurements — and build reliable methods plus a habit of sanity-checking answers.

  • 10 weeks
  • Working adults
  • 3 hours a week
  • Examples from your job

Program 04 · Pass the math section

GED & placement tests

These tests cover a wide range shallowly, which is good news: a diagnostic shows exactly which few topics are costing you points. We drill those, practice under time, and work on the test-day pacing that trips up prepared people.

  • 12 weeks
  • Test booked or planned
  • 5 hours a week
  • Official practice tests
