Why the gap is never where you think

People arrive wanting help with algebra. The gap is usually four years earlier, and that's good news.

How we work

Find the gap, fill it, build up

Math is cumulative in a way that few subjects are. A missing piece from years ago quietly breaks everything built on top of it, and no amount of practicing the harder topic fixes the foundation.

So we diagnose first. It takes one session, and people are often relieved: there's a specific, findable reason the subject stopped making sense, and it isn't a lack of ability.

After that it's short daily practice and honest feedback on your method rather than just your answers. Adults who practice ten minutes a day make progress that surprises them.

Our principles

Three beliefs behind the method

Start at the gap

Wherever it is. Skipping back is the fastest route forward.

Ten minutes daily

Frequency beats duration for building skills.

No embarrassment

Adults only, small groups, and any question asked as often as needed.
