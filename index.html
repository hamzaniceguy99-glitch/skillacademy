How we work

Nobody is bad at math

Math builds on itself more strictly than any other subject. Miss three weeks with pneumonia in seventh grade and the next five years arrive in a language you never learned — which feels exactly like being bad at it.

SkillAcademy works with adults only, one to one or in very small groups. There's no class to fall behind in, nobody watching you hesitate, and no limit on how many times you can ask why.

Frequently asked

The questions we get most

I'm 45 and can't do fractions.

That's an extremely common starting point here, and not a reflection of your intelligence. Adults usually move faster than teenagers because they know why they're there.

Will I be in a class with teenagers?

No. We work with adults only, one to one or in small adult groups.

I get panicky doing math.

Math anxiety is real and well documented. We work slowly, without timed pressure at the start, and build confidence before speed. It isn't therapy, and if anxiety goes well beyond math we'll suggest speaking to a professional.

Can you get me through the GED math section?

We prepare you thoroughly, but no honest tutor guarantees a pass. We'll tell you plainly whether your test date is realistic.

How long until I catch up?

It depends on where the gap starts and how consistently you practice. Ten minutes a day matters more than any single session.

Can I cancel anytime?

Yes. No contract, no minimum term. Access stays open until the end of the month you already paid for.

Where did math stop making sense?

Thirty minutes, free and one to one, to find out. No test and no judgment.
